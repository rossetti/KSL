Usage & don'ts

  • Tiers: Primary (clean) is the default and the only version below ~200px. Expanded (labeled) is for large formats only — the banner, slides, the social card, print.
  • Clear space: keep padding of at least the cap-height of the "K" around the whole mark.
  • LinkedIn: the banner (1584×396) uses the brand; keep your headshot as your profile photo — people follow people.
  • Don't recolor the amber, stretch the mark, add shadows or gradients, or use the labeled version small.
  • Final production tip: convert the "KSL" text to outlines so the wordmark renders identically on machines without Helvetica/Arial.
